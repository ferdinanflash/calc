Plan Comparison
Tier Overview
Strategy Notes
  • Cheapest per RFC: 1 conversion per day, all half price (7/week) - about 6.9 FC per RFC, but only ~10 RFC.
  • Tier 1 only (20/week): 330 FC for ~29 RFC (11.4 FC/RFC) regardless of how the 20 are split, as long as all 7 daily discounts are used.
  • Going past Tier 1: do the bulk on Day 1, then 1 per day, so the discounts land on the expensive slots (e.g. 26/week = 540 FC for ~41.9 RFC).
  • Tiers 4-5 cost 2.7-3.1× more FC per RFC than Tier 1 at full price; use them only when you need RFC fast.
RFC costs and probabilities are based on Crystal Laboratory community data. Daily pattern uses 50 FC/day. Tier 3–5 cost more FC per RFC than Tier 1. Outcomes are random; results are estimates only.

How to play

Tap an uncovered tile to move it into one of the 7 Storage Zone slots. Three identical tiles clear automatically. Clear the entire board with an empty Storage Zone to advance.

Withdraw returns the last tile placed. Reconstruct shuffles the remaining tiles. Remove moves up to three currently accessible tiles into Storage.
